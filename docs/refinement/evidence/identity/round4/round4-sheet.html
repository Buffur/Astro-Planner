Culmina — C4 Orbit C, round 4 (S13.1)

The owner kept C4 Orbit C and liked R1, R3 and R4. This round reworks them with the principles in README.md:

  • one construction for all: orbit centre, star axis at 40°, an opening symmetric about it with equal clearance to both ends, inner orbit at 0.6 R on the same axis;
  • a curved four-point sparkle instead of the straight-sided star;
  • optical centring: the ink centroid (red cross) sits 1 unit above the canvas centre, so the C is not left-heavy;
  • Android adaptive icon: the mark inside the 66 dp safe zone (outer dashed circle) and above the 48 dp minimum (inner dotted circle); a one-colour layer;
  • checked down to 16 px, one colour on black and on white, the themed icon, the splash, and with the name.

R1 · Balanced

The orbit, an inner orbit at 0.6 of its radius on the same axis, and a white sparkle in an opening sized for equal clearance. Extent 32.2 of 33 (≈ 64 dp mark, inside 48–66 dp).

R3 · Ice star

R1's construction with the ice-blue sparkle, drawn about 7 % larger because a lighter-luminance colour carries less visual weight than white. Extent 32.6 of 33 (≈ 65 dp mark, inside 48–66 dp).

R4 · Crescent orbit

A tapered orbit (2.6 → 9.0 → 2.6) on the same axis and opening; the white sparkle. Extent 32.4 of 33 (≈ 65 dp mark, inside 48–66 dp).

R4b · Crescent with orbit

R4's taper with R1's inner orbit and R3's ice star: the inner orbit keeps it from reading as a plain crescent Moon and star. Extent 31.9 of 33 (≈ 64 dp mark, inside 48–66 dp).
