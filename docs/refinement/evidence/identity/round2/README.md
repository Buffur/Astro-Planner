# S13.1 round 2 — Culmina logo concepts

> **Status:** 2026-10-17, five concepts for the owner's choice of a direction (P9.4: the agent does not
> pick). Round 1 (`../`, V1–V4) was **not approved**; the owner asked for clearly different concepts:
> navy palette, calm and technical, close in spirit to Siril or Stellarium (clean, minimal, scientific,
> not playful), strong small-size readability, a usable monochrome layer, and freedom to leave the
> arc/window/star construction. After the owner picks a direction, a refinement round follows; the
> approval of the final artwork is recorded in DECISIONS E.1 (S13.1 acceptance 4).

## Files

- `C1`–`C5` `-colour.svg`: the adaptive foreground on night navy `#0B1230` (108-unit canvas).
- `C1`–`C5` `-monochrome.svg`: the one-colour themed-icon layer.
- `concepts-sheet.html` and `concepts-sheet.png`: each concept with its rationale, the full canvas, the
  launcher's 72 dp under circle and rounded-square masks, 48/32/24 px, the themed icon on light and
  dark wallpapers, and the mark with the name "Culmina".

Palette: navy `#0B1230`, light `#E6EBF7`, muted blue `#7F8DC0`, ice-blue accent `#8CCBFF`, star white.
Every concept fits the 66 dp safe zone (extent ≤ 33 units from the centre).

## Concepts

| | Concept | Symbolises | Why it fits Culmina | Small sizes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| C1 | **Meridian** — circle, horizon chord, meridian, a star on the meridian | A star at culmination: crossing the meridian, highest in the sky | The name's literal meaning in the textbook construction of the sky; calm, scientific | Ring and star read at 24 px; the thin meridian becomes a hint |
| C2 | **Reticle** — ring, four ticks, centred star | The target chosen and framed | Capture-software language (Siril, N.I.N.A.); the most technical | The strongest silhouette at 24 px |
| C3 | **Quadrant** — altitude quadrant with a scale, a sight line and a star | Measuring a star's altitude; culmination is the maximum | Heritage instrument; precise, elegant; asymmetric and distinctive | Ticks merge at 24 px; the quarter-circle and star remain |
| C4 | **Orbit C** — an open orbit forming a C, the star in its opening, an inner orbit | The C of Culmina; the star rising to its high point | A lettermark that pairs naturally with the name | Very legible; the inner orbit vanishes below 32 px by design |
| C5 | **Star trails** — arcs of equal angle around the pole, one ending in a bright star | A long exposure: astrophotography itself | Instantly "night-sky photography"; calm and circular | Weakest at 24 px: the thin trails become texture |

Not based on the round-1 arc/window/star construction: C2, C3 (partly), C4, C5.
