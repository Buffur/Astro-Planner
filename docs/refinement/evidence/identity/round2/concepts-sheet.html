Culmina — logo concepts, round 2 (S13.1)

Five clearly different directions for the owner's choice (P9.4: the agent does not pick). Palette: night navy #0B1230, light #E6EBF7, muted blue #7F8DC0, ice-blue accent #8CCBFF. Each concept has a one-colour layer for the themed icon; nothing depends on colour. Round 1 (V1–V4) was not approved.

C1 · Meridian

Idea. A star crossing the meridian of the celestial sphere: the moment of culmination, when a target stands highest and an imaging window is best. The circle, horizon and meridian are the textbook construction of the sky, so it reads as scientific and calm.

Small sizes. Ring and star survive at 24 px; the thin meridian fades to a hint there, which is acceptable. Safe-zone extent 26.8 of 33.

C2 · Reticle

Idea. A focusing and framing reticle with the target star centred: the target is chosen, placed and planned. Close to the visual language of capture software (Siril, N.I.N.A.); the most technical of the set.

Small sizes. The strongest silhouette at 24 px: a ring, four ticks, a centre mark. Safe-zone extent 32.8 of 33.

C3 · Quadrant

Idea. The astronomer's quadrant: the historic instrument for measuring a star's altitude. Culmination is the highest altitude, and the app plans around it. Precise and elegant, with a heritage feel; asymmetric, so it differs from common round app icons.

Small sizes. At 24 px the scale ticks merge into the arc; the quarter-circle and the star still read. Safe-zone extent 31.2 of 33.

C4 · Orbit C

Idea. A lettermark: an open orbit forms the C of Culmina, and the star rises into its opening at the upper right, the culmination point. The inner orbit adds depth without detail. The most brand-like and the easiest to pair with the name.

Small sizes. Very legible: one thick open ring and a star; the inner orbit disappears below 32 px by design. Safe-zone extent 29.2 of 33.

C5 · Star trails

Idea. Star trails around the celestial pole: the signature of a long exposure, so it says astrophotography at once. Every trail spans the same angle, as in a real exposure; one ends in a bright star, the object being planned. Calm, circular, and recognisable to anyone who has shot the night sky.

Small sizes. At 24 px the thin trails become a texture around one bright star; weaker than C2 or C4 at that size. Safe-zone extent 30.8 of 33.
