## 2. Audit of round 3's R1, R3 and R4

- **Star:** a straight-sided four-point star (generic "sparkle" clip art); its tips were not related to
  the ring ends, so the gaps to the two ends differed visually.
- **Balance:** the ring was centred on the canvas, but the mass of the C sits left and low and the
  star sits upper right: the ink centroid was off the optical centre (P5).
- **Construction:** the inner orbit's opening was unrelated to the outer one; stroke 6.5 looked heavy
  on navy (P6).
- **R4:** an elegant line, but it reads as a crescent Moon with a star, a widespread symbol (P2).

## 3. Applied in round 4

- One construction: the orbit centre, the star axis at 40°, an opening symmetric about it and sized
  so both ring ends clear the star equally; the inner orbit at 0.6 R on the same axis (P4).
- A curved four-point sparkle (quadratic sides through a pinched centre) (P1, P2).
- Optical centring: the whole mark moved until its ink centroid sits 1 unit above the canvas centre
  (P5).
- Strokes 6.0; the ice star enlarged (P6, P7).
- R4b: R4's taper with R1's inner orbit and R3's ice star, to break the crescent reading (P2).
- Each variant checked: the safe zone and the 48 dp minimum (P3), 16–48 px (P8), one colour on
  black and on white, the themed icon, the splash and with the name.
