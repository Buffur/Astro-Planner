follows for the bundled CC BY-SA 4.0 data, the dependencies' licences, the store listing and the

## 4. GPL-3.0 against the requirements

| Requirement | GPL-3.0 |
| --- | --- |
| 1. Free | The owner may distribute it free of charge. **Met**, for the owner's own copies. |
| 2. No monetisation | **Not met.** Anyone may sell copies (GPL FAQ), and the owner cannot add a no-sale term. |
| 3. No modification without permission | **Not met.** The GPL grants everyone the right to modify and share modified versions. |

So GPL-3.0 does not meet requirements 2 and 3. **No open-source licence can:** the Open Source
Definition forbids both restrictions (criteria 3 and 6). Meeting them means a **source-available** or
**proprietary** licence, which is no longer "free software" in the FSF/OSI sense.

## 5. The options

| Option | Requirement 1 | 2 | 3 | Consequences |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **G. Keep GPL-3.0** | yes | no | no | Nothing changes; About and `COMPLIANCE.md` stay. Others may sell or modify, under the GPL's copyleft (their changes must stay GPL and share their source). |
| **N. PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0** (future versions) | yes | yes | **no** | Modification and redistribution stay allowed for noncommercial use. Source-available, not open source. |
| **S. PolyForm Strict 1.0.0** (future versions) | yes | yes | yes | Noncommercial use only; no distribution, no changes. The source can stay public to read. Not open source. |
| **P. All rights reserved** (future versions), the app distributed free as a binary | yes | yes | yes | Only the owner distributes; the source may be private or published "for reference" with no licence. Not open source. |

## 6. Consequences common to N, S and P

1. **Copies already shared stay GPL-3.0.** Every version published under the GPL (including the
   public repository's history) keeps its licence for everyone who received it (GPL FAQ; the right
   "cannot be withdrawn"). A change applies to future versions only.
2. **Who can relicense.** Relicensing needs the agreement of every copyright holder of the code. The
   git history records two author names (the owner's handle and a second name); whether the second
   is the same person or another contributor, and the status of AI-assisted changes, are questions
   for the owner and, if needed, a lawyer.
3. **The catalog stays CC BY-SA 4.0.** The adapted OpenNGC data must keep BY-SA (or a compatible
   licence) with no added restrictions (§3(b)), whatever the app's licence. Whether the catalog can
   ship inside an app under N, S or P as a separately licensed part, or must be downloadable apart,
   **needs legal advice**; its notice must stay in About either way.
4. **Dependencies.** The direct dependencies are permissive (MIT, BSD, Apache-2.0) and allow use in a
   source-available or proprietary app, provided their notices ship (the licence page does).
   Transitive dependencies should be checked once before a change.
5. **Open-Meteo.** Its free tier already forbids subscriptions and advertising; requirement 2 is
   consistent with it under every option.
6. **The store.** Google Play accepts GPL, source-available and proprietary apps alike; the listing's
   licence statement, About and `COMPLIANCE.md` change with the choice.
7. **Wording.** Under N, S or P the app should no longer say "free software" or "open source"; it can
   say "free of charge" and name the licence.

## 7. What P9.5 does after the owner decides

- **G:** nothing changes (RG-12 closed as "keep").
- **N, S or P:** the new licence text in `LICENSE` (the steward's text, unchanged), a note that
  earlier versions remain GPL-3.0, About's licence paragraph, `COMPLIANCE.md`, the store listing, and
  a check of the catalog's handling against the answer to §6.3. No licence text is written by the
  agent.
