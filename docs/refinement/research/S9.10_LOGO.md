## 2. What does not work in the current execution

The concept (a target's altitude arc over the horizon, its imaging window marked, a star at the
top) is the owner's and is kept in every alternative. The execution has five problems:

1. **The star floats.** The arc `M28,70 Q54,22 80,70` peaks at y = 46, but the star spans y = 24–38:
   it hangs eight units above the curve, so at launcher size it reads as a separate dot rather than
   "the target at culmination".
2. **Three stroke weights** (horizon 3, arc 5, window 7). The window bulges out of the arc like a
   blob, and the 3-unit horizon is about one pixel on a 48 px icon, so it vanishes.
3. **The themed icon is the colour drawing tinted.** Overlapping shapes merge; the window, the one
   idea that makes the mark specific, disappears in the Android 13+ themed mode.
4. **The weight sits high.** The content spans y = 24–70 on a 108 canvas, centred about 7 units
   above the middle, leaving the lower third empty inside the mask.
5. **Error red.** `#FF4D3D` is the colour the platform uses for errors; on a navy ground a small red
   stroke also reads as a notification dot.

## 3. Alternatives (all keep the vector concept)

Each is a 108-unit adaptive foreground on the current navy (`#0B1230`); every shape stays inside
the 66 dp safe zone (a circle of radius 33 around 54,54; checked for each option, round caps
included). The arcs are quadratic curves; the window is the same curve from t = 0.3 to 0.7, and the
star is centred on the peak. Colours are proposals; the owner may mix a shape from one option with
a colour from another.

## 4. The themed icon (every option)

A dedicated `drawable/ic_launcher_monochrome.xml`: the arc and the star as one silhouette, the
window as a **gap-free thicker segment** (A, C, D) or the filled area (B), no overlapping strokes
of different colours. The system tints it; nothing depends on colour.

## 5. The splash (proposal)

- **Android 12+:** the chosen foreground as an `AnimatedVectorDrawable` in
  `windowSplashScreenAnimatedIcon`: the arc draws from the horizon to its end (trim path, about
  400 ms), then the window appears (about 150 ms). Android shows it only until the first frame, so it
  never delays startup, and it caps the animation (1 000 ms); with animations turned off in the
  system the icon is shown still.
- **Before Android 12:** the static icon, as today.
- **In the app:** no extra splash screen; Tonight's startup indicator stays (a fake progress bar is
  excluded by P9.4).
