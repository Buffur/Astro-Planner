# AstroPlan Feature Status

> **S7.2a, 2026-09-29 (equipment, F-23; ADR-020 §2, RG-11 = C1):** a rig has a camera type (Phone · DSLR or mirrorless · Astro camera (colour) · Astro camera (mono) · Unknown), chosen in the rig editor and Unknown by default; the example rig stays Unknown; "Add from a photo" never proposes one. It feeds no calculation yet; S7.2b uses it to offer the right capture settings. Schema v20.

> **S7.1, 2026-09-29 (the planner F-40, NPF guidance F-26; RD-08 = T3):** a plan can override its rig's tracking for its night ("Tracking for this plan", under the rig; S6.9's "Set the tracking for this plan" opens the same choice). The guidance uses the effective value; the rig and the example rig's Unknown are never changed; Save records the value and its source; Discard, Copy, New plan and Open handle it; the export carries it. Schema v19. The rig editor's field reads "Tracking (default for plans)".

> **S6.V1, 2026-09-28 (the capture plan, F-40; S6V-01, TD-082):** Undo after deleting a block puts that block back at its place and keeps whatever was added or edited since; the "Example plan" badge comes back only when nothing else changed since the delete, so an edited plan is never shown as the example. After New plan, Copy, Open or Track live, an old delete's Undo changes nothing and says "Not undone: the capture plan was changed again since." A saved plan's snapshot is never touched.

> **S6.16, 2026-09-28 (the planner F-40, Tonight F-47, Night & Moon; the owner's corrective pass before the Stage 6 validation: TD-075 to TD-080, ADR-019 §6 amended):** **The planner's order** is now the status, then site ▾ · night ▾ (on a card), the target, the rig, the capture plan, then "Tonight for this target" (the chart and the windows, as supporting analysis; shown once a target is chosen, or the no-site card), then the conditions (Night & Moon, Weather, sky darkness). The status card has an edge in the verdict's colour and shows its numbers (Integration, Capture ends) as label and value; Fill or Trim is an outlined button; the section headings are in the scale's section-heading size. **Missing inputs** have their own reason: "Needs a site" says the site is needed for the night and the target's windows, "Needs a target" says to choose one to see tonight's windows; the empty plan's "no light frames" appears only once a site, target and rig exist (TD-075; planner and Tonight). **Undo** follows Fill or Trim ("Filled Ha 300 s to 375 frames · Undo"), a block edit saved in its dialog ("Block changed: … · Undo") and "Start from the example plan" ("Started from the example plan · Undo"): it restores the blocks and the example badge exactly, as an ordinary edit (a saved plan becomes Saved · changed; its saved snapshot is untouched), and after another block edit it changes nothing and says "Not undone: the capture plan was changed again since." (TD-079; no global undo, no history). **The √N graph** names the plan's point, "Your plan: 100 frames · 10.0x", beside a dot drawn like the graph's, and its end "For comparison: 200 frames · 14.1x"; √N and the points are unchanged (TD-076). **Tonight** has its title as the page's header, the site and night on a card with a place and a date icon, "Your plan" as the card's heading above the target and rig (TD-077), Open planner as the card's filled button in every state, and, without a target, "Choose a target — Any object in the catalogue" and "What can I image tonight? — Candidates for this site and night, by usable time" in the card, with the second no longer repeated in the actions below (TD-080). **Night & Moon** no longer repeats its summary: the Sun section ("Sun and twilight") lists sunset, the twilights and sunrise, and the Moon section gives the illumination and the closest approach; the dark span and the Moon's up-times are in the summary once (TD-078).

> **S6.14, 2026-09-28 ("What can I image tonight?", F-51; RD-10 = O1, UX-29):** the default order is usable time, then frame fill (how much of the frame the target fills with the chosen rig; unknown sorts last), then the name, so targets that all have the whole dark window no longer fall back to alphabetical order. The header names the order ("sorted by usable time, then frame fill") instead of "(no score)"; the other sorts are unchanged. Sorting only; no score.

> **S6.13, 2026-09-28 (Tonight, F-47; UX-10, UX-11, UX-13, UX-17, UX-24; TD-054):** Tonight leads with the plan. > **S6.12, 2026-09-28 (the planner's night and opportunity timeline, F-40; P6.11, UX-08):** the altitude chart is evolved into the night and opportunity timeline: the night's state at the user's darkness limit (bands without seams, with a thin line where one gives way to the next, so they stay apart in field mode), the target, the imaging windows, the Moon and "now" as before, and a dashed "Capture ends" line from the fit when it measured the plan.
